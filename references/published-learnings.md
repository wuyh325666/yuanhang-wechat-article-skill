## 2026-05-29 Codex Desktop Pet Article

Published URL:

- https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/S6yXRoOGKibjnGaemG-TVA

Published title:

- Codex桌面宠物：把AI任务状态养成一只小东西

What the final published version shows:

- The WeChat title field carries the article title; the body starts directly with the opening paragraph. Do not duplicate the Markdown H1 in editor HTML by default.
- The opening works better when it reaches the real value quickly: "desktop pet looks like fun, but it is really task-status feedback."
- A 远航收藏夹 draft should not be all one-line paragraphs. Use short lines for emphasis, but merge explanatory sentences into complete paragraphs.
- User likes direct tool-value framing: not "cute feature news", but "what problem this solves and how to use it."
- Images are part of the final article flow. Insert screenshots or visuals after important sections, with centered captions.
- Strong headline fit: concrete object + functional metaphor, e.g. "把AI任务状态养成一只小东西".
- The final article body kept left-border numbered headings, yellow highlights, 15px body text, and image border styling.

Reusable revision rule:

After drafting, do a "published-readiness pass":

1. Remove repeated title from body.
2. Tighten first screen.
3. Merge unnecessary one-line sentences.
4. Add image placeholders where the reader needs visual confirmation.
5. Check that tutorial steps are explicit enough to follow.
